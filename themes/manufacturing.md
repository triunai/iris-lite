# Manufacturing

## Thesis

No manufacturing-activity data logged yet. Flash PMIs for July (Eurozone and US) are due 24 Jul and are explicitly flagged as this run's key watchpoint — the first real read on whether the oil shock is passing into activity and prices.

## Supporting Evidence
- **2026-08-04 (ref Jul):** US ISM Manufacturing **55.6** vs 54.0 consensus, 53.3 prior. Production 52.2->58.5, employment 49.7->52.8 (back to expansion), new export orders 48.5->53.0 (back to expansion), backlog 50.5->55.0, imports 52.9->55.7. Customers' inventories "too low" at 40.7% against a rising backlog - the configuration that pulls production forward (inference). *(ISM via PR Newswire, rel. 3 Aug 10:00 ET)*

## Contradicting Evidence
- **2026-08-04 (ref Jul):** China RatingDog manufacturing PMI **50.9**, a four-month low, missing 51.9; output and new orders at four-month lows. *(Investing.com, 3 Aug)*
- **2026-08-04:** Iron ore ~US$94/t, its lowest in more than a year (from ~$110 three months prior) - the industrial-demand read disagrees with the US survey. *(IBTimes AU, 3 Aug 14:20 AEST)*
