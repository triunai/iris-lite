# Foreign Exchange

## Thesis

No single dollar-direction story: USDJPY sits at its weakest since 1986 (a standalone dislocation tied to BOJ policy lag vs. Fed), while the dollar index itself is roughly flat/soft — meaning oil-exporter currencies (like MYR) and hike-repricing elsewhere (EUR, prospective JPY) are offsetting a simple 'risk-off = long dollar' read.

## Supporting Evidence

USDJPY ~163.2, weakest yen since 1986 (23 Jul); USDMYR firmed to 4.0850/0890 from 4.0870/0910 on oil-linked support (23 Jul, 6pm close); Bloomberg Dollar Spot Index roughly flat/soft.

## Contradicting Evidence

A classic risk-off session would typically see broad dollar strength; that did not clearly happen here.
