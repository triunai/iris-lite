# Monetary Policy

## Thesis

The oil-driven inflation shock has revived a hawkish repricing across three major central banks simultaneously — an unusual synchronised shift. Markets are now pricing a real probability of a Fed hike this year (previously not the base case), the ECB explicitly kept a September hike on the table despite holding at 2.25% on 23 Jul, and BOJ officials are reportedly open to moving faster ahead of their 30-31 Jul meeting.

## Supporting Evidence

Fed 2026 hike odds ~71% per Polymarket (via Yahoo Finance, 23 Jul); ECB held at 2.25%, unanimous, but Lagarde: 'the burden of proof is on data' re: September (23 Jul, Euronews/Central Banking); BOJ officials reportedly open to faster hikes (23 Jul, Saxo).

## Contradicting Evidence

No new core-inflation print has yet validated a hike anywhere — the hawkish repricing is running ahead of confirmed data.
