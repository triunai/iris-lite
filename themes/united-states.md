# United States

## Thesis

Two forces hit US equities simultaneously on 23 Jul: the global oil/inflation shock (pushing yields to an 18-month high and reviving Fed-hike odds) and idiosyncratic AI-capex disappointment (Alphabet's raised spending guide, Tesla's weak print). Underlying labour data stayed resilient (claims beat), which is itself double-edged — good for growth, but removes any 'Fed will rescue us' cushion if inflation data turns.

## Supporting Evidence

S&P 500 -1.00% to 7,424.24, Nasdaq -1.86% to 25,212.45, Dow -0.96% (23 Jul, Yahoo Finance); UST 10Y ~4.70% (highest since Jan 2025); US initial jobless claims 187k vs. ~210k consensus; VIX >19 (+~15% d/d).

## Contradicting Evidence

Defensive/idiosyncratic strength persisted alongside the selloff — Lockheed Martin +11.3%, Super Micro +19.8% — showing this was not indiscriminate risk-off.
