# Europe

## Thesis

Europe is facing the same imported-energy shock as everywhere else, but with a central bank that just pivoted more hawkish than expected (ECB held at 2.25% but explicitly opened a September-hike door) — a stagflation-adjacent combination worth tracking closely. Sector-level, the session split sharply: technology fell hard (STMicro guidance shock), while energy and defense/aerospace names were bid.
