# Earnings

## Thesis

Earnings season is exposing a split inside 'AI capex' as a theme: fundamentally strong results (Alphabet's cloud growth) are being punished for guidance (a raised $195-205bn 2026 capex plan), while Tesla missed on results with negative free cash flow. Meanwhile Super Micro (+19.8%) and Korea's Kospi (semis-led, +3%) ran counter to the broader tech selloff — the market is not uniformly bearish on AI capex, it's discriminating.

## Supporting Evidence

Alphabet raised 2026 capex guidance to $195-205bn (GOOG -6.9%, 23 Jul); Tesla -12.3% on a weak print with negative free cash flow; STMicro guided Q3 revenue below expectations (-15% in Europe, 23 Jul).

## Contradicting Evidence

Super Micro Computer +19.8% and Kospi +~3% (semis-led) on the same day — a clear counter-signal to a uniform AI-capex scare.
